# Daily Loop Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 9, 2026**

Daily Loop (the "App") is a routine-management service operated by an individual developer. We value your privacy and publish this Privacy Policy to comply with applicable data protection laws, including South Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1) At sign-up (login)
- **Google Sign-In**: email address, Google account identifier, basic profile information provided by default by Google (name, profile photo URL, etc.)
- **Kakao Sign-In**: Kakao account identifier (unique ID) only
  - We do not collect your email via Kakao login. Kakao does not permit the email consent scope for apps published by individual developers, so the App only requests the "nickname" consent item.

### 2) Information you provide while using the App
- Routine titles, schedule/repeat settings, completion records, memos, attached photos
- Diary entries, calendar memos
- Favorites and routine collections (presets)
- YouTube video links you add and their metadata (title, thumbnail, channel name)
- **Text you enter into the "Add routine with AI" feature**: used to automatically analyze your sentence and convert it into a structured routine (title, time, repeat rule, etc.).

### 3) Automatically collected information
- None. The App does not include any advertising or analytics SDKs.

We do not collect your name, phone number, home address, location data, or payment information.

## 2. How Information Is Collected

- When you sign in with your Google or Kakao account
- When you create or enter routines, diary entries, memos, or "Add routine with AI" text within the App

## 3. Purpose of Use

- Identifying members and maintaining login sessions
- Storing and syncing your routines, records, and memos across devices
- Calculating and displaying statistics (completion rate, streaks, etc.)
- Providing the "Add routine with AI" feature (converting your input sentence into a structured routine)
- Scheduling in-app notifications (slot reminders, etc.) — notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device; no additional data is sent to a server for this purpose.
- Responding to service-related inquiries

## 4. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information or disclose it to third parties, except as described in "5. Processing Outsourcing and Cross-Border Transfer" below, to the extent necessary to operate the service.

## 5. Processing Outsourcing and Cross-Border Transfer

To operate the App reliably, we outsource certain processing to the providers below. For some of them, your data is transferred to and processed on servers located outside Korea.

| Processor | Purpose | Cross-border transfer | Country | Data transferred |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase, Inc. | Database, authentication, photo file storage | Yes | United States | Your full account and app-usage data |
| Anthropic, PBC | Analyzing the text you enter into "Add routine with AI" (Claude API) | Yes | United States | Text you enter into the AI parsing feature |
| Google LLC | Google account sign-in authentication, in-app YouTube video playback (embedded player) | Yes | United States | Account authentication info / data processed by YouTube (Google) under its own policy during video playback |
| Kakao Corp. | Kakao account sign-in authentication | Domestic only | South Korea | Kakao account identifier |

- **When and how data is transferred**: Data is transmitted over the internet at the moment you use the relevant feature.
- **Special notice for the AI parsing feature**: Text you enter into "Add routine with AI" is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. It is used solely to convert your input into a routine — please avoid entering sensitive personal information (health data, passwords, etc.).
- For details on Google/YouTube's practices, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Retention Period

- If you request account deletion, your data is deleted without delay.
- When you delete a routine or a collection (preset), it is not immediately purged — to preserve historical completion records, it is kept for **2 weeks** before permanent deletion (you can restore it from the "Restore Routines" screen within this window).
- Custom video categories you create and delete are kept for **3 days** before permanent deletion.
- Text you enter into "Add routine with AI" is used only transiently for the conversion request and is not separately stored on our servers.
- Information subject to mandatory retention under applicable law is retained for the required period.

## 7. Deletion Procedure and Method

- **Procedure**: Upon account deletion or a deletion request (or after the retention period above elapses, where applicable), the relevant data is deleted without delay.
- **Method**: Electronic files are deleted using methods that prevent recovery.

## 8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time.

- You can edit or delete your own routines, records, and memos directly within the App.
- **Full account deletion**: An in-app self-service deletion feature is under development. To request deletion in the meantime, please email us at the address below — we will verify your request and delete your account and associated data without undue delay (typically within a few business days).

## 9. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

## 10. Security Measures

- Database access is restricted using Row Level Security so that only you can access your own data.
- All network communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
- API keys and other sensitive credentials are kept only in server-side, private environment variables and are never bundled into the app.

## 11. Cookies and Local Storage

The App does not use web cookies, advertising identifiers, or analytics tools that track your behavior. Your login session and display preferences (theme color, font, language, etc.) are stored only in local storage on your device and are not transmitted elsewhere.
